What is the Scientific Calculator?

A scientific calculator handles advanced maths beyond basic arithmetic — trigonometry, logarithms, exponents, roots and constants like π and e. This free online scientific calculator gives students, engineers and scientists a full-featured calculator in the browser.

How it works

The calculator evaluates expressions using standard order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS): brackets first, then exponents, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. Functions such as sin, cos, tan, log and √ apply to their arguments before the surrounding operators.

Trigonometric functions can operate in degrees or radians, exponents use xy, and logarithms include base-10 (log) and natural log (ln), where ln(e) = 1.

Order of operationsEvaluating 2 + 3 × 42 gives 2 + 3 × 16 = 2 + 48 = 50, because exponents and multiplication are applied before addition.
TrigonometryIn degree mode, sin(30°) = 0.5 and cos(60°) = 0.5, while √(144) returns 12.

When to use it

  • Solving algebra, trigonometry and calculus homework.
  • Engineering and physics calculations involving powers and roots.
  • Converting between degrees and radians for angles.
  • Any quick computation needing logs, exponents or constants.
